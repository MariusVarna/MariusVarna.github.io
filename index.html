Conference Paper · ICIST 2026

Interpretable Classification of Skin Lesions using ProtoPNet and Expert Feature Integration

Most medical AI tells you what it thinks — not why. Our paper, accepted at ICIST 2026, trains a prototype-based classifier on HAM10000 to do both: matching state-of-the-art accuracy on seven lesion categories while exposing its decision logic in terms dermatologists can actually audit. Built on ProtoPNet, anchored to clinical features from the ISIC 2018 dataset. Final version in progress.
